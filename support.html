Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a feeding?

Tap the Feeding card on your dashboard and select the feeding type (breast, bottle, or solid food). Enter the amount or duration and tap Save. All logs are stored in your timeline for easy review.

How does sleep tracking work?

Open the Sleep tracker and tap Start when your baby goes down. Tap Stop when they wake up. DadPro will record the duration and build a sleep history so you can spot patterns over time.

Where can I find milestone tracking?

Go to the Milestones section from the main menu. You'll see age-appropriate developmental milestones listed by category. Tap a milestone to mark it complete and add notes or a photo.

How do I use the vaccine tracker?

The Vaccines section shows the recommended immunization schedule based on your child's date of birth. You can log each vaccine as it's given and view upcoming doses so you're always prepared for your next pediatric visit.

What is the DadPro AI Coach?

The AI Coach is your on-demand parenting assistant. Ask it questions about sleep schedules, feeding amounts, developmental stages, or anything on your mind as a new dad. It provides personalized guidance based on your child's age and your logged data.

How do I delete my account or data?

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, simply navigate to the More menu on the DadPro App, scroll down to Settings, and press Delete Account.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email info@beadadpro.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are always helpful. We take bug reports seriously and aim to address them quickly.
